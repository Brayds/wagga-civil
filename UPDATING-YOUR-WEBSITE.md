# Updating your website — the one-pager

Your website has a built-in AI assistant that makes changes for you. You describe what you want in normal words; it does the work, checks nothing is broken, and publishes. Changes are live at **waggacivil.com.au** about two minutes later.

## What you can change yourself

- Phone numbers, email, business details
- Service areas (add or remove towns)
- The words on any page — services, about, homepage
- Add new job photos
- Add, remove, or reword services
- Update the equipment/fleet list

**What's a job for Brayden:** anything about how the site *looks* (colours, fonts, layout, logo), new pages, or anything the assistant tells you to send his way. Just text him.

## How to make a change

1. Open the **Claude** app on your computer.
2. Open the **wagga-civil** folder (it will remember it after the first time).
3. Type what you want, starting with `/update-site`. For example:

> `/update-site` Angus has a new number — 0400 123 456

> `/update-site` Add Albury to the areas we service

> `/update-site` I've got 3 photos from the Riverside Estate job in my Downloads — add the best one to the bulk earthworks page

> `/update-site` We sold the 3.5 tonne excavator and bought a 5 tonne — update the fleet list

4. Answer any question it asks, then let it finish. It will tell you when the change is live.

## Good to know

- **Photos:** AirDrop them from your phone to the computer first (they land in Downloads), then just tell the assistant where they are.
- **Wait 2 minutes** after it says "publishing", then check waggacivil.com.au. Refresh the page if you still see the old version.
- **You can't really break it.** Every change is checked before it goes live, and Brayden can wind anything back to exactly how it was.
- **If anything looks wrong on the site**, don't try to fix it yourself — text Brayden and he'll sort it.

## One-time setup (done with Brayden — you don't need to remember this)

- Claude account (yours, ~$30/month) + the Claude desktop app
- A free GitHub account (this is where the website's files live)
- The website folder copied onto your computer

That's it. If you forget everything else, remember: **open Claude, type `/update-site`, say what you want in normal words.**
